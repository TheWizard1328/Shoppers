# For you

## How Notes help

Notes turn useful details from your conversations and connected data into a lasting workspace your Superagent can keep current.

Use them to track projects, people, decisions, preferences, deadlines and follow-ups without searching through old conversations.

## Example briefing

* [ ] Review the renewal quote before Friday

* [ ] Confirm the venue for next month's offsite

* **Decision needed:** Choose a launch date before the team meeting
